About the Project

It showcases my experience in learning design, training delivery, capability development, knowledge management, and performance improvement across enterprise support operations and cloud technology.

The portfolio demonstrates how I translate complex technical, operational, and business processes into structured learning experiences that help people build knowledge, develop practical skills, and perform their roles more effectively.

My Approach to Learning

My approach is performance-led rather than training-led. I begin by identifying the problem, analysing its root cause, and determining whether the appropriate response is training, coaching, improved documentation, process clarification, or another operational intervention.

My learning and improvement cycle follows these stages:

Identify → Analyse → Design → Develop → Deliver → Assess → Reinforce → Measure → Improve

This approach supports practical learning, demonstrated competency, knowledge retention, and continuous improvement.
